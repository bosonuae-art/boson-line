About this sheet

Scoring. A point for every game you call right, same as Dad’s spreadsheet. Vegas is the third player, and its pick is the preseason favorite off the 2026 sheet, locked before kickoff so its record can’t drift.

The line. The Line column is the current DraftKings number by way of ESPN, with the move off the preseason number underneath. Against-the-spread is judged on that current line.

Results. Final scores arrive with the odds. Hand entry is only for fixing something ESPN gets wrong. Games lock at kickoff, and while the sheet is sealed you can’t see the other column on a game until you’ve picked it yourself.

Sources. Schedule, preseason lines and byes come from Pete Holland’s 2026 workbook. Kickoffs, live lines, totals, scores, club marks and team colors come from ESPN. Club marks are used to identify the teams and belong to their clubs.
