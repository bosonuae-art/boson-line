About this sheet

Scoring. A point for every game you call right, same as Dad’s spreadsheet. Vegas is the third player, and its pick is the preseason favorite off the 2026 sheet, locked before kickoff so its record can’t drift.

The line. The Line column is the current DraftKings number by way of ESPN, with the move off the preseason number underneath. Against-the-spread is judged on that current line.

Staying current. The strip under the week shows what is on right now, how old the numbers are, and whether both sheets are in step. It refetches on its own — every 20 seconds while a game in the week you are looking at is live, every ten minutes otherwise, and whenever you come back to the tab. Refresh forces it.

Sharing. Both of you write to the same sheet, so a pick shows up on the other phone within a second or two and the other person’s newest change is highlighted as it lands. Picks made with no signal are held on the phone and sent the moment it reconnects — the line reads “Everything saved” only when the shared sheet really has them.

Results. Lines and final scores are pulled live from ESPN whenever you open a week, so nothing here needs republishing. Hand entry is only for fixing something ESPN gets wrong. Games lock at kickoff, and while the sheet is sealed you can’t see the other column on a game until you’ve picked it yourself.

Sources. Schedule, preseason lines and byes come from Pete Holland’s 2026 workbook. Kickoffs, live lines, totals, scores, club marks and team colors come from ESPN. Club marks are used to identify the teams and belong to their clubs.

Show a sample season Fills the sheet with an invented week 10 — a season in progress, results and all — so you can see how it looks in November. Your real picks stay exactly where they are, and nothing from the sample is saved or shared. Adding ?demo=1 to the address opens straight into it.
